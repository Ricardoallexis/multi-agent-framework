# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Multi-Agent Framework aims to provide a constructive, technically focused environment for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience to collaborate.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, and observable behavior;
- provide enough technical context for others to understand and reproduce a problem;
- accept reasonable review feedback and acknowledge uncertainty when appropriate;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, licensing, and intellectual-property boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, spam, and deliberately disruptive behavior;
- avoid publishing credentials, private data, security-sensitive details, or third-party restricted material.

Disagreement about technical design is normal. Keep discussions evidence-based and oriented toward improving the project.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed collaboration spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may limit participation when behavior materially disrupts the project or violates these expectations. Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and focused on maintaining a safe and productive project environment.

For sensitive conduct concerns, do not post private details publicly. Contact the repository maintainer through an appropriate private GitHub contact channel when one is available.
